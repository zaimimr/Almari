# Live closet scan Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** She props the phone up (or someone holds it), holds up one garment or bag at a time, and the app takes a full-resolution photo of each one by itself once it has been still for about 0.7 seconds. Every photo goes through the Part 4 pipeline while she keeps scanning, and she reviews the pieces on the existing Keep and Drop screen at the end.

**Architecture:** A native camera view in `modules/closet-vision` (`LiveScan.swift`) runs an `AVCaptureSession` with a preview, a video data output and a photo output. At a fixed rate it parses one frame with its own instance of the Part 4 clothes parser (no person masking, so a held piece outside her outline still counts), finds hands with Vision hand pose, and sends a coarse grid to JavaScript: one ATR class and one average colour per cell, plus hand points and timings. All decisions are pure TypeScript in `src/domain/scan.ts`: the baseline of what she wears, the diff that finds a held piece, hands at its edges, the stillness timer, the duplicate check and the speed readout. When the domain says capture, the screen asks the native view for a photo; the view plays a tone and a haptic, saves `<id>-original.jpg`, parses the photo around the held box and writes a region cutout. The screen then adds an import job with the scan's `captureId` and that region (or the box as a crop when the parser finds nothing), and the existing import runner prepares it in the background.

**Tech Stack:** Expo SDK 57, expo-router, Expo Modules native view (`View(LiveScanView.self)`), AVFoundation, Vision (`VNDetectHumanHandPoseRequest`), Core ML (the SegFormer B2 clothes parser from Part 4), AudioToolbox, UIKit haptics, TypeScript with `tsx --test`.

**Spec:** [planning/implementation/LABELS-CAPTURE-STYLING.md](../implementation/LABELS-CAPTURE-STYLING.md), section "Part 9: Live closet scan". Pipeline: [2026-10-01-04-capture.md](2026-10-01-04-capture.md). Shared contracts: [2026-10-01-00-overview.md](2026-10-01-00-overview.md).

- No comments in code of any kind.
- No attribution lines in commits or files.
- Every user-facing string through `t()` with an English and a bokmål entry. `npm run strings` passes.
- Minimum code, existing style, Prettier on changed files.
- No photo leaves the phone. Frames are never stored; only captured photos are written, into `closet-photos`.
- Held garments and bags only: the parser classes upper, dress, skirt, pants, hat and scarf, and bag. Shoes, belts and sunglasses are never captured; they use the photo flow.
- `closet.v3` stays. Scan jobs are ordinary `ImportJob`s with `captureId` and `region` or `crop`, so no data change.

## Decisions

- **Frame rate:** `scanFps = 4` in `src/domain/scan.ts`, passed to the native view. The parser on the Neural Engine is expected to take 40 to 90 ms per frame on an iPhone 16 Pro; 4 frames per second leaves room for the import runner preparing captured pieces at the same time and keeps heat down. The value is a constant so it can be tuned from the readout.
- **Grid:** 36 by 48 cells (3:4, the photo preset's aspect). Labels are sampled from the 512 by 512 parse at cell centres; colours are a Lanczos downscale of the frame.
- **Baseline:** taken when a person covers at least 8% of the grid for 1 second. Start again takes a new one.
- **Held piece:** cells whose class is a held class and whose class or colour (CIEDE2000 above 18) differs from the baseline. The largest 4-connected group is the piece. It must cover at least 3% of the grid and at least 15% of her baseline outline (4% for bags), and at least one hand must be within 8% of the box edge.
- **Still:** the box overlaps the box at the start of the hold by an intersection over union of 0.75 or more and its colour stays within 8. After 700 ms it is captured.
- **Duplicate:** a held piece of the same kind within colour difference 10 of the previous capture is not taken again. After preparation, an embedding very close to an earlier photo in the same scan gets the existing Same piece or Different piece question; `findDuplicate` now compares jobs in the same capture when their photos differ.
- **Speed readout:** frames per second and median parser time over the last 12 frames, shown in development builds, and in any build after a long press on the status line.
- **Front camera:** frames are mirrored like the preview; the saved photo is not mirrored, so the box is flipped before the cutout.

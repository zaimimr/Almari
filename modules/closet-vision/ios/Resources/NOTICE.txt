GarmentEncoder is derived from SigLIP 2 (google/siglip2-base-patch16-224) by Google, licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). It was converted to Core ML and its weights were quantized to 8 bits.

ClothesParser is derived from mattmdjaga/segformer_b2_clothes, a fine-tuned NVIDIA SegFormer B2, licensed under the NVIDIA Source Code License for SegFormer (https://github.com/NVlabs/SegFormer/blob/master/LICENSE) for non-commercial use only. It was converted to Core ML and its weights were quantized to 8 bits.

The outfit compatibility weights in src/domain/scoring/compat-head.json were trained on the Polyvore Outfits dataset by Mariya I. Vasileva, Bryan A. Plummer, Krishna Dusad, Shreya Rajpal, Ranjitha Kumar and David Forsyth (ECCV 2018), licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Item images were turned into SigLIP 2 embeddings and only the trained weights are included.

SAM2_1SmallImageEncoderFLOAT16, SAM2_1SmallPromptEncoderFLOAT16 and SAM2_1SmallMaskDecoderFLOAT16 are SAM 2.1 Small model weights from Meta, distributed as Core ML packages by Apple at https://huggingface.co/apple/coreml-sam2.1-small.
